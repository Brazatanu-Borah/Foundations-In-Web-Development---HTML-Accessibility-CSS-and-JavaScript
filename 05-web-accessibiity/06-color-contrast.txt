{RELATED TO PEOPLE THAT CAN SEE}
================================

Use accessible 'COLOR SCHEMES' >> must pass accessible color


Be aware of Color Contrast ( between primary text and background)
  - max contrast (pure black with pure white)
  - #222 with #eee (good contrast)


Keep in consideration for 'Color-Blindness' issue in some people
  >> different types of color blindness
  >> red and green (can't separate these colors apart)


Design decisions must be made with color contrast in mind
  - diff for diff people (kids, old people, good eyed people, poor eyesight people)
